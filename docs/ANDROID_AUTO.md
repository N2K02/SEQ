# Android Auto

SEQ has no car media browser of its own. Music from any app played through Android Auto (Spotify, YouTube Music, …) is processed by SEQ Auto's output-mix effect. When Android Auto puts the phone in car mode, SEQ switches to its car balance (road-noise bass/treble lift, no volume-based loudness because the head unit controls the level).

Verify on a head unit or the Desktop Head Unit that projection audio passes through the output mix on your device.
